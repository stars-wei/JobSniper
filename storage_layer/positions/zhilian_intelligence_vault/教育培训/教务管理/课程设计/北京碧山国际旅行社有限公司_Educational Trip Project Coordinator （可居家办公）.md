# 职位详情：Educational Trip Project Coordinator （可居家办公）

## 01. 核心原始描述 (JD)

Responsibilities:

• Work directly with English-speaking clients to design customized experiential education programs.

• Work closely with the Project Manager and Operator to create tailored proposals based on client requirements.

• Share destination knowledge with internal teams and contribute to marketing initiatives.

• Continuously innovate and enhance program offerings to keep our portfolio fresh and engaging.

• Collaborate with the operations team to handle any emergencies that arise before, during, or after the program.

• Prepare all necessary program documents and monitor program status.

• Participate in programs on the ground at least 2-3 times per year to gain firsthand logistical insights.

Requirements:

• Proficient in both spoken and written English.

• Strong client service orientation with excellent written and verbal communication skills.

• Ability to multitask and remain composed in a fast-paced environment.

• Detail-oriented with strong organizational skills.

## 02. 公司背景
碧山WildChina不是一家旅行社，她更是一个健康生活态度的倡导者。公司的创始人张玫来自云南，毕业于哈佛商学院，曾就业于麦肯锡公司。但她以自己的生活为画板，画出了一条常人不走的路。作为国内领军的旅游公司， 碧山WildChina第一个带客人转了云南的梅里雪山，第一个把某咨询公司的高管会议从酒店大厅挪到了杭州西湖边掩映在荷花间的茶室，第一个带中国客人跟国家地理的摄影师同行去不丹。这一切，就是为什么碧山WildChina在2009年被《国家地理》评选为 - “世界顶极探险旅行公司”之一。详见：www.wildchina.com www.beshan.com
